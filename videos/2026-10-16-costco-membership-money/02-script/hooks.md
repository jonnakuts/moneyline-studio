# Cold-open hooks considered

Chosen (in script.md):
> Picture a Costco cart… Under 2% of Costco's money comes from that card. But it's as big as about half of
> Costco's operating profit.
Why: concrete object (cart vs card), a big legible gap (2% vs half), visual from the first second.

Runners-up:
1. "Costco would be an ordinary shop, if it weren't for a $65 piece of plastic." (strong, but the number
   gap lands later)
2. "On every hundred dollars you spend at Costco, it keeps under two dollars. So how does it make billions?"
   (good question, weaker image)
3. "Costco often sells you its goods before it has even paid for them." (surprising, but a side point, not
   the thesis)
